### Section 1: Performance-Based Questions (PBQs)

#### PBQ 1: Threat Actor Attribution & Incident Profiling Matrix

**Scenario:**
You are a Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) analyst reviewing five recent security incidents across multiple industry sectors. Your task is to populate the attribution matrix by matching each incident summary to the correct **Threat Actor Type**, **Access Location**, **Capability / Resource Level**, and **Primary Motivation**.

**Incident Summaries:**

1. **Incident A:** Forensic investigators discover a custom, previously unknown kernel-level rootkit deployed inside a defense contractor's network. Telemetry indicates the actor maintained silent persistence for 14 months, carefully exfiltrating propulsion system blueprints over encrypted DNS queries during normal business hours without disrupting operations.
2. **Incident B:** On the morning of an international trade summit, a multinational agricultural corporation's public web portal is defaced with slogans protesting genetically modified organisms, accompanied by a volumetric Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack that takes customer-facing services offline.
3. **Incident C:** A hospital network is hit with a coordinated double-extortion ransomware campaign. The attackers exfiltrate 4 TB of patient records, encrypt all hypervisors, and direct executives to a dark web portal featuring a live 24/7 customer support chat to negotiate a cryptocurrency payment.
4. **Incident D:** During an annual cloud expenditure audit, the security team discovers that the marketing department used a corporate credit card to subscribe to an unvetted third-party generative AI and SaaS analytics platform, uploading confidential customer email lists to meet a tight campaign deadline.
5. **Incident E:** A senior database administrator who recently received a negative performance review configures a scheduled cron job using their legitimate administrative credentials. The script is programmed to drop all production transactional tables and overwrite backup volumes 30 days after the administrator leaves the company.

**Selection Pools:**

* **Threat Actor Types:**
  * Nation-State / APT

  * Internal

  * Very High (State-sponsored funding, zero-day research)
  * High (Professional syndicates, commercialized infrastructure)
  * Moderate / Variable (Crowd-sourced tools, open-source frameworks)
  * Authorized Privilege / Institutional Knowledge
  * Non-malicious / Operational Bypass

* **Primary Motivations:**
  * Espionage / National Security
  * Financial Gain / Extortion
  * Political / Philosophical Ideals
  * Sabotage / Revenge
  * Productivity / Convenience (Unintentional Risk)

#### PBQ 2: Log Triage & Threat Actor Differentiation

**Scenario:**
Review the three threat intelligence briefs below and answer the analytical questions that follow.

> **Brief 1: Alert ID #4092**
> Perimeter firewall logs show a single residential IP address firing thousands of automated exploit attempts against an enterprise web server over a 10-minute window. The payloads consist entirely of unmodified, publicly available Metasploit modules targeting Windows XP and Apache 1.3 vulnerabilities from over a decade ago, even though the target server runs modern containerized Linux.

> **Brief 2: Alert ID #5118**
> A mid-sized solar panel manufacturer loses a major municipal contract after a rival bidder submits a proposal priced exactly 1.5% lower with identical technical guarantees. Post-incident forensics reveal that an external contractor's compromised VPN account was used exclusively to download the folders `Q3_Bid_Pricing_Final.xlsx` and `Supply_Chain_Margins.pdf` three days before the bidding deadline.

**Tasks:**

1. For **Alert ID #4092**:
   * **a.** Identify the **Threat Actor Type**.
   * **b.** Explain which specific indicator in the brief rules out an Advanced Persistent Threat (APT).
2. For **Alert ID #5118**:
   * **a.** Identify the **Primary Motivation** behind the intrusion.
   * **b.** Identify the secondary action taken (`data exfiltration`, `service disruption`, or `destructive wiping`) to achieve that motivation.

### Section 2: Scenario-Based Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs)

#### Question 1
A software development team is frustrated by the two-week turnaround time required by the corporate IT department to provision new virtual machines. To avoid project delays, the lead developer sets up an independent public cloud account, connects it to the corporate Git repository using personal access tokens, and deploys a test database containing real production schemas.

Which of the following best describes this risk?

* A. Advanced Persistent Threat (APT)
* B. Shadow IT
* C. Hacktivism
* D. Script kiddie activity

#### Question 2
A cybersecurity firm tracks a threat group that exclusively targets semiconductor fabrication plants and energy grids. The group utilizes custom-built malware signed with stolen hardware vendor certificates, chains multiple zero-day vulnerabilities, and operates over multi-year timelines without ever demanding ransom payments.

Which threat actor category and primary motivation pair is most consistent with this profile?

* A. Organized crime motivated by financial gain
* B. Nation-state / APT motivated by espionage and strategic disruption
* C. Unskilled attacker motivated by notoriety
* D. Insider threat motivated by competitive advantage

#### Question 3
A pharmaceutical startup discovers that its proprietary drug trial formulas and executive recruitment lists were covertly copied to an external storage bucket. Two months later, a rival pharmaceutical company announces an identical compound synthesis technique and poaches three of the startup's lead biochemists.

Which of the following motivations best explains this attack?

* A. Philosophical objection
* B. Competitive advantage
* C. Hacktivism
* D. Thrill and curiosity

#### Question 4
An enterprise SOC observes repeated port scans and noisy SQL injection attempts against its public e-commerce site. Upon analyzing the HTTP requests, the analyst realizes the attacker is running a free, point-and-click vulnerability scanner downloaded from a public forum without configuring target parameters, resulting in broken payloads and immediate WAF blocks.

Which type of threat actor is responsible for this activity?

* A. Unskilled attacker / script kiddie
* B. Malicious insider
* C. Nation-state operator
* D. Organized crime syndicate

#### Question 5
A manufacturing firm terminates a plant supervisor following a workplace dispute. Later that evening, the supervisor uses a shared legacy engineering password that had not yet been rotated to log into the Industrial Control System (ICS) portal and intentionally recalibrates the assembly line robotic arms, causing physical damage to equipment and halting production.

Which combination of actor and motivation is demonstrated in this scenario?

* A. External hacktivist motivated by political ideology
* B. Shadow IT motivated by financial gain
* C. Insider threat motivated by sabotage
* D. Organized crime motivated by data exfiltration

#### Question 6
A collective of anonymous individuals targets a major financial institution after the institution announces funding for a controversial mining project. The group leaks internal executive emails to the press to embarrass leadership and coordinates volunteer botnets to overwhelm the bank's online login portal.

How should the SOC classify this threat actor and their motivation?

* A. Organized crime; Financial gain
* B. Hacktivist; Political or philosophical beliefs
* C. Nation-state; Espionage
* D. Insider threat; Competitive advantage

#### Question 7
A threat intelligence report describes a highly structured cybercriminal syndicate that operates like a corporate enterprise, complete with a human resources department, affiliate revenue-sharing programs, dedicated exploit brokers, and money-laundering specialists.

Which primary motivation drives this type of threat actor?

* A. Ideological activism
* B. Financial gain
* C. Patriotism and national security
* D. Faster internal workflow deployment

#### Question 8
During a threat modeling exercise for a defense logistics agency, the CISO wants to evaluate threat actors based on their core attributes. Which of the following attribute sets accurately characterizes an Insider Threat compared to an External Organized Crime group?

* A. Insider threats always possess higher funding and zero-day exploit research capabilities than organized crime groups.
* B. Insider threats inherently operate from an internal access position with pre-existing authorized trust, bypassing many perimeter controls.
* C. Insider threats are exclusively motivated by political philosophy, whereas organized crime is motivated by sabotage.
* D. Insider threats rely solely on off-the-shelf scripts, whereas organized crime never utilizes data exfiltration.
